# 🚀 Quick Start - SharanyaVerse

Get your portfolio running in 5 minutes!

## 📋 Immediate Steps

### 1. Open the Website (Right Now!)
```bash
# Just double-click this file:
index.html
```

Your portfolio is already live locally! 🎉

### 2. What You'll See
- Navigation bar with your name "SharanyaVerse"
- Hero section with introduction
- About section with your background
- Skills showcase
- Two featured projects
- Contact section
- Everything works, but uses placeholders

### 3. Three Things to Update First

#### A. Your Contact Info (5 minutes)
Open `index.html` and find these lines (around line 180):

```html
<a href="mailto:your.email@example.com" ...>
<a href="https://github.com/yourusername" ...>
```

Replace with:
```html
<a href="mailto:YOUR_REAL_EMAIL@gmail.com" ...>
<a href="https://github.com/YOUR_GITHUB_USERNAME" ...>
```

#### B. Your Profile Photo (2 minutes)
1. Find a good photo of yourself
2. Rename it to `profile.jpg`
3. Put it in `assets/images/` folder

#### C. Your Resume (1 minute)
1. Save your resume as PDF
2. Rename it to `Sharanya_Mukherjee_Resume.pdf`
3. Put it in `assets/resume/` folder

---
